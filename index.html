Understanding a Portfolio

A portfolio is a collection of financial assets held by an individual or organisation. Depending on the investor, it may include stocks, bonds, cash equivalents and digital assets.

Risk and Diversification

Diversification involves spreading investments across different assets. It may help manage exposure to individual investment risks, although it cannot eliminate risk entirely.
